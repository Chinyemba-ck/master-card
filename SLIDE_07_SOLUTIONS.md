## The Solution: CareSignal

Think of CareSignal as a **credit score for rural healthcare small businesses** — it measures billing efficiency, reimbursement capture rates, and claim denial rates, giving lenders the financial visibility to fund providers and keep them open.

### Why Healthcare Businesses?

* **Largest employer sector** — 21% of Winnsboro's workforce
* **93.5% of residents are insured** — the customer base exists; providers are financially fragile
* **RHTP $208M to Louisiana (Dec 2025)** + **Rural Small Businesses Act (Senate committee March 2025)** both fund exactly these businesses — CareSignal delivers the outcome data they require

---

## How It Restarts the Local Economy

**Step 1 — Stabilize healthcare providers**
Clinics stay open → jobs remain in the community

**Step 2 — Childcare centers stay open**
Parents can return to work → Labor Engagement rises

**Step 3 — Workforce participation rises**
Household income increases → Personal Income rises

**Step 4 — Families stay and spend locally**
Housing occupancy stabilizes → Net Occupancy rises → local businesses recover

---

## Key Message

> **CareSignal stabilizes healthcare businesses and restarts the chain:
> Childcare → Work → Income → Local Economic Recovery.**

CareSignal is not creating new demand. It is keeping the businesses that already employ 21% of Winnsboro's workforce financially visible to lenders — and letting the same chain that raised Archibald's IGS by +9 in one year fire in Winnsboro.
